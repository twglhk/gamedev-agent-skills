# Trailer beat sheets

Adapted from Derek Lieu's structures (social 90 s, line of rising action, announce, MVP Steam trailer), Zukowski's 30 s breakdown and Valve's guidance. The example beats are for a deckbuilder auto-battler with a horror boss and a night-to-dawn win condition; map them to your own core verbs and turn. Every beat uses only what is in the build.

## A. Steam gameplay trailer, 75-90 s (slot 1 once you have breadth)

| Time | Beat | Picture | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2 | Thumbstopper | A late-game fight mid-climax: full team, a signature power hits the boss, HUD on, readable muted | music at full energy or one hard hit |
| 2-8 | Genre in one loop | Card pick → the unit joins the formation → the fight resolves | drops on actions |
| 8-15 | Hook | Your differentiator, shown in-shot | stopdown or riser into it |
| 15-25 | The verbs | Each core verb, one shot each, 2-3 s | a trough: room for one short line or none |
| 25-55 | Escalation | Combos growing; the biggest chain with clear cause → effect; pressure rising, a unit falls | build; one silence + sting at about 40-45 s |
| 55-60 | Low point | The darkest moment: the team nearly wiped | near-silence, a drone or heartbeat |
| 60-75 | Climax | The turn; the team holds; breadth montage (units, enemies, places), cuts speeding up | biggest section, cuts on hits |
| 75-80 | Button | One memorable last image | last hit, cut to black |
| 80-90 | Title + ask | Title, one ask that fits (follow / wishlist / play the demo) | music tail, clean cut |

Same shape in Save the Cat terms (`save-the-cat` skill): opening image → catalyst → break into two → fun and games → midpoint (false victory) → all is lost → finale → final image.

## B. 30 s cutdown (first Steam trailer, social, showcases)

| Time | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0-3 | The most going on: a late-game team mid-fight |
| 3-7 | The hook revealed in-shot (a camera move or a reveal) |
| 7-15 | One loop of the core verbs |
| 15-20 | The antagonist and its pressure |
| 20-25 | Breadth and the payoff (a glory shot) |
| 25-30 | Title + one ask |

The 15 s gameplay-only version (some showcases ask for one) = rows 0-20 compressed, no text.

## C. Announce trailer, 45-75 s

Leave one or two ideas people repeat ("the game where the hero was hiding in a diner"). Settle into the familiar genre in the first third to half, then turn it with the hook, a quick content montage, end fast. Announce trailers get embedded in articles for years.

| Time | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0-5 | An in-engine image that sets the tone; gameplay first if it will sit in Steam slot 1 |
| 5-15 | The ordinary world; something wrong |
| 15-25 | Hook 1 |
| 25-35 | Hook 2 (or the antagonist) |
| 35-55 | Gameplay montage of the core loop |
| 55-65 | Button, title, one ask |

If it is cinematic, it goes in slot 2 and a gameplay cut takes slot 1.

## D. Next Fest / demo trailer, 45-60 s

The shape of B, stretched to show the demo's first 20 minutes honestly, ending on "play the free demo now" (and the demo's end date if it has one). Swap it into slot 1 for the fest week and back afterwards. The fest also needs the store page and a trailer live before the registration deadline, the demo public about 10 days before (press get the list then), and a tested livestream plan.

## E. MVP gameplay trailer, 90-180 s (an early Coming Soon page)

Raw-ish gameplay in loop order, 3-5 s shots so a scrubbing shopper lands on something, HUD on, variety from the midpoint, no logos or non-interactive cinematics. Valid when you have variety but no hero trailer yet.

## F. Microtrailer audit (for whichever trailer is in slot 1)

Scrub ten random points. Each 1 s must show HUD and a recognisable action. Cut fades, title cards and near-black frames down to almost nothing.

## Where sources disagree, and a default

| Question | Default |
| --- | --- |
| HUD on or off | On for Steam slot 1 and Next Fest; selective or off in announce and social hype shots |
| First trailer length | 30 s first; 75-90 s once there is breadth; 90-180 s MVP only with real variety |
| How fast gameplay appears | ≤ 2-3 s on Steam; an announce trailer may open on an in-engine tone image ≤ 5 s |
| Title cards | Few, short, on hits, about feelings |
| Gameplay vs voice-over for a deckbuilder | Gameplay first, with a line that frames the twist; growth comes from the demo and streamers |
