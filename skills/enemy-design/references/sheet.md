# Enemy sheet: the fields

The fields a full enemy draft fills, in order. Card enemies fill only what `SKILL.md` § Card enemy lists. One enemy per document. No numbers in any field; write the data path instead. Mark the draft as unreviewed until the human has seen it.

## Bones

- Name: provisional until the human picks from candidates.
- Family, family mark, weight, role (one from your role list).
- Core fantasy: one or two sentences.
- Core action: the one strong act (SKILL.md step 3).
- Three tone words, most important first.
- The question it asks (SKILL.md step 2).
- Weapon, 2 to 3 attack verbs, range, behaviour loop.
- Your project's extra fields (stage, place in the enemy deck, what it hits).

## Shape

- Motif and period reference.
- Family mark on the body.
- Colour role, inside the art bible.
- 2 to 3 silhouette words.
- 3 to 5 fixed elements.
- The shape that reads from across the board.

## Body and nature

- Origin and want.
- Body reason, senses, stages and variants.
- Telegraph, weakness and strength.
- Sound, smell, motion.
- What we never show.
- How it behaves when mixed with other enemies.

## Person

- Body facts: height, build, face, hair, scars.
- Costume and build-up: layering order, materials, head, torso, arms, legs.
- Three habits: while waiting, just before attacking, when falling.
- One line or a cry.
- Back story.
- People and bosses: personality, want, wound, weakness, contradiction, voice. Monsters: instinct and the trace of who they were.

## Lore paragraph

One novel-style paragraph a reader who doesn't know the game can picture, with the core action at its centre.

## Bosses and mid-bosses only

- Thesis: one emotion and its contradiction.
- What each phase says.
- How the board changes.
- Villains with a will: the eight steps in [villain.md](villain.md).

## Art direction

- Put in.
- Leave out (overlap warnings and the bible's reject list).
- 2 to 3 references.

## End of the draft

- The branches you dropped while writing, one line each, so the human can pick one if they scrap the draft.
- After review: the card or boss checks and their results.
