# Marketing methodology: evidence behind the playbook

Researched October 2026. Steamworks rules are first party. Wishlist and Next Fest numbers come from Chris Zukowski's developer surveys (observational and self-selected), not from Valve. Social platforms publish no reliable universal best times or conversion rates, so treat everything here as a starting point to test.

## 1. Wishlist funnel

- Put up a Coming Soon page once the pitch and look are stable. A long lead time has no real downside unless the game changes enough to confuse early wishlisters. [Valve: Coming Soon](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/coming_soon) · [Valve: Wishlists](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist)
- Wishlists notify players at release and discounts and feed a few lists (Popular Upcoming). Valve says they're mostly not a factor in algorithmic visibility; purchases and play are. [Valve: Visibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility)
- There's no dependable public page-view-to-wishlist rate; track your own. [HTMAG benchmarks](https://howtomarketagame.com/benchmarks/)
- First-week wishlist-to-sale medians (older Carless data via Zukowski): about 15% under 5k wishlists, 20% at 5k to 40k, 23% at 40k to 100k, 25% above. Not a forecast.
- Next Fest, February 2026 survey (about 170 to 182 games): median 806 wishlists earned (30th pct 382, 70th 1,839, 95th 13,461). Pre-fest wishlists correlated strongly with fest wishlists (ρ = 0.825). Demo conversion (played and wishlisted ÷ players) median about 16%, 70th pct about 20%. Demos out longer before the fest did about 2.5× better than ones released under a month before. [HTMAG Feb 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/04/13/making-sense-of-the-february-2026-steam-next-fest/) · [HTMAG Next Fest benchmarks](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/26/benchmarks-how-many-wishlists-can-i-get-from-steam-next-fest/)
- Next Fest visibility starts randomized and then personalizes on behavior; correct tags and a live demo are prerequisites. [Valve: Next Fest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest) · [tips](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest/tips)
- No robust X-post-to-wishlist benchmark exists. Use Steam UTM links and traffic reports to attribute. [UTM](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/utm_analytics) · [Traffic](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/traffic_reporting)
- Indie X data (howtomarketagame.com, 2026-05-07, with Indie Game Joe): posts that make people feel something spread; horror spreads best among indie genres; open video on the strongest moment; about 7 wishlists per 1,000 views is the median once a Steam page exists.

Priority: a clear Steam page, then shareable gameplay proof, then a demo you keep iterating, then creators, press and festivals, then steady social discovery.

## 2. Post types and why each works

| Type | Why it can work | Example hook | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gameplay proof | Shows the core loop and a player choice at once | "One card. Three doomed heroes. One more round." | 8 to 20 s, 16:9 or 4:5, captions, clean loop |
| Before/after | Visible progress, invites feedback | "The enemy used to float. Here's the fix." | Side by side 1:1 or 4:5, labelled |
| Card reveal | A specific effect is easier to talk about than a feature | "Would you take a wound to replay this?" | 3 to 5 slides, one card each |
| Hero spotlight | Ties a mechanic to a character | "She can't kill it. She can keep it busy." | 4:5, one character, one mechanic |
| Enemy behavior | A readable threat makes the world and combat legible | "It learns the pattern you used last round." | 8 to 15 s, cause then consequence |
| Build choice | A real choice invites opinions and shows replay variety | "Survive now or hit harder later?" | 2 to 4 options, UI large enough to read |
| Devlog | Shows authorship, gives something specific to answer | "Why the heroes act on their own, and where your choices matter." | 150 to 300 words or a 3 to 6 post thread |
| AI process, candid | A distinctive story, but it can eclipse the game | "The agent wrote the first draft; here's what I threw away." | Real artifact, the human review, what shipped |
| Atmosphere | A memorable image earns attention without lore | "The radio keeps reading tomorrow's casualty list." | 5 to 12 s clip or one image, gameplay context clear |
| Milestone | A timely reason and one clear action | "The demo is live." | Short clip, date, one link |
| Real question | Feedback on a real problem, not a request for praise | "Does this enemy's intent read before the turn resolves?" | Screenshot or clip plus one question |

Mix game evidence, process, atmosphere and asks. Not every post asks for a wishlist.

## 3. Card news on X

- The first frame is the hook; each next frame adds one piece of evidence or one step; the last can carry the CTA.
- 3 to 5 panels is a starting test, not a rule (X allows 4 images per post). If it needs six slides, make a clip.
- 4:5 (1080×1350) fills the phone feed; 1:1 for squares; 16:9 for gameplay. Check the live crop before posting.
- One headline and at most two short lines per slide; large type, high contrast, margins; game UI readable on a phone.
- Add alt text. Don't stuff hashtags or repeat links. [X Help: photos](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/posting-gifs-and-pictures)

## 4. Reddit

- No safe universal self-promotion ratio exists. Reddit's spam policy forbids repetitive or unsolicited promotion, and each sub's moderators add rules. Read the live rules and pinned posts right before posting. [Reddit: Spam](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam)
- Titles: true, specific, descriptive, and say that you made the game. No "please wishlist", no bait, no posing as a player.
- Removal risks: cross-posting the same thing, link-only posts, promo comments on unrelated threads, vote manipulation, multiple accounts, unsolicited DMs, ignoring flair or megathreads.
- Keep your own table of target subs with their AI policy, self-promo rules and posting days, dated, and recheck it at publish time.
- Since November 2025 Reddit no longer hands out self-service API keys (Responsible Builder Policy), so plan on posting by hand.

## 5. X

- Timing: test a few fixed windows for the audience you want, then compare qualified visits over several posts.
- Hashtags: zero to two relevant ones; event tags like `#screenshotsaturday` only when the post fits the event.
- One self-contained clip or image goes out as a single post; a sequence that teaches becomes a short thread.
- Links: once a Steam page exists, test the link in the post against the link in the first reply, with UTM.
- Reply to comments on your posts as the developer; never mass-reply a pitch. [X Rules: platform manipulation](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/platform-manipulation)

## 6. Brand and AI

- Build recognition around the game and the developer's judgment: one title treatment, palette, type, frame and motif, and one candid solo-dev voice.
- Steam's AI disclosure covers AI content shipped to players (pre-generated and live), not productivity tools. Say what was generated, what a human reviewed or changed, and what players will see. [Steamworks content survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey)
- AI talk can overshadow the game and raise doubts about originality and quality. Lead with playable proof; use process posts to show human decisions and rework.

## 7. Posting through a real browser

- A logged-in browser is not a policy exemption. Platform rules and limits are hard constraints. [X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation) · [Reddit User Agreement](https://www.redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement)
- The human approves the exact account, text, media, link and sub. No autonomous posting, commenting, voting, following or DMs.
- Low volume at a person's pace; no bursts, duplicates or repeated cross-posts. Stop on any warning, CAPTCHA, rate-limit notice or odd account state.
- No proxy rotation, account cycling, fingerprint spoofing, CAPTCHA solving or ban evasion. A suspended account loses its audience and history.

## 8. Measuring

- Log per post: time, channel or sub, type, hook, media and ratio, link or UTM, views, engagement, profile visits, link clicks, Steam visits, wishlists, moderation outcome. Note festivals, creator coverage and page changes. Steam's wishlist report updates daily, excluding the latest day. [Valve: Wishlist reporting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/wishlist/reporting)
- Primary metrics: attributable wishlists per Steam visit, and wishlists per post.
- Repeat a format only after several comparable posts beat your own baseline. Change one variable at a time.
- High engagement but few clicks: the post entertains but creates no intent, or the ask is weak. Many visits but few wishlists: the page, the promise, the audience or the demo is off.
- Pause a format that keeps underperforming, draws the wrong audience or gets removed. Under about 300 visits a rate is a feeling, not a number.
