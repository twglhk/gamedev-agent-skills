# Short formats

Examples are written for a deckbuilder auto-battler with a horror boss you survive until dawn. Swap in your own game's verbs, threat and win moment; keep the timing.

Seconds are for a native 9:16 Short; X and Reddit cuts may run longer (up to 45 s) by holding beats, not by adding ideas. "Text" is an on-screen line in the post's language. Every format needs approved, in-game material that is actually in the build; if a mechanic on the beat sheet isn't playable yet, that format waits.

Evidence: what worked for other games (cases in `video-craft` [sources.md](../../video-craft/references/sources.md)). Your own numbers replace these after 8-10 posts per format.

## F1. Cold-open climax (default)
- 0-1.5 the peak: the team's big hit lands on the boss, a big number pops. No text.
- 1.5-4 the boss shrugs it off or regenerates. Text: "It can't die."
- 4-9 the fight goes on; the win meter creeps. Text names the goal ("Survive until dawn.").
- 9-12 the win moment hits, the threat recoils. Hard cut.
- Risk: if the boss's reaction is subtle at phone size it reads as "nothing happened".

## F2. Number goes up
- 0-1 a small hit, "12".
- 1-6 the chain: each trigger adds; the counter speeds up; the SFX pitch climbs a step per tick.
- 6-9 the final multiplier slams; hitstop, shake.
- 9-10 loop back to the "12" frame.
- Risk: reads as a Balatro copy unless the clip shows **why** it multiplied (the icons that fire, in order).

## F3. Synergy reveal "A + B"
- 0-2 card A alone does something weak.
- 2-4 card B alone, weak.
- 4-5 both on the board; a 0.5 s held beat of silence.
- 5-11 the combined effect sweeps the enemy line.
- 11-13 freeze on both cards. Text: "Together."
- Best on X and Reddit, where people pause to read. Risk: card text unreadable on a phone; show art and icons, one combo per clip.

## F4. Building the team (pick → fight)
- 0-1.5 three cards fan in; one is picked.
- 1.5-5 two more fast picks, each with silhouette and a one-word power (≤ 1.2 s each).
- 5-12 the three fight on their own; the second pick saves the first.
- 12-14 the win.
- Risk: menus are low energy. Keep picks fast and the fight long.

## F5. Hidden in plain sight
- 0-2 four ordinary characters. Text: "One of them is a hero." (or whatever your hidden thing is)
- 2-7 clue beats, one per 1.5 s.
- 7-9 the unlikely one is revealed.
- 9-14 that character in a fight.
- Invites "I knew it" comments. Only once the discovery mechanic is real in the build.

## F6. Chaos escalation
- 0-2 one enemy, one unit, calm.
- 2-10 every 2 s the screen doubles: more enemies, more units, more effects.
- 10-13 the peak; the win moment; everything freezes.
- Risk: unreadable chaos. Keep one focal colour (e.g. the player units' rim light) throughout.

## F7. The wipe
- 0-2 a strong team, confident. Text: "Perfect team."
- 2-7 one boss move undoes it; units fall one by one, a sting on the last.
- 7-9 silence, black, a heartbeat.
- Feeling: dread, then the itch to retry. Risk: reads as "unfair"; post F8 next.

## F8. Clutch finish
- 0-1 the clock near the end, one unit left on 1 HP. Text: "One left."
- 1-8 the last card fires; a narrow survival.
- 8-10 the win condition lands as the boss's blow falls short.
- 10-12 the survivor's silhouette, held.
- Must be a real run (fixed seed), never staged; staged clutches get called out.

## F9. "Would you play this?" (X and Reddit, 30-45 s)
- 0-3 the strongest moment; 3-15 one loop of the core verbs; 15-35 escalation; 35-45 breadth (units, enemies, places).
- Post text (≤ 50 words): one line of what the game is, one real question. Follow or link in a reply.
- Works only if the clip itself is strong; the template is familiar.

## F10. The game in 15 seconds
- 0-3 a big hit; 3-7 a pick; 7-12 the boss phase; 12-14 the win; 14-15 hard cut.
- A Japanese X trend format (2025); use it as a shape, not a hashtag.

## F12. Comment reply
- 0-1 the comment on screen (it must be a real one).
- 1-12 the answer shown in the game.
- The cheapest follow-up to any clip that got traction; YouTube has "reply with a Short".

## F13. Tone clash
- 0-3 the game's bright, heroic side, heroic sound.
- 3 a hard sting: colour drains, sound drops to a drone.
- 3-10 the horror rises in the same frame.
- 10-12 the hero turns to face it. One short goal line.
- A strong setting hook for any game with two contrasting tones. Keep it in-game; too polished reads as a trailer.

## F14. Micro loop (6-8 s)
- One action whose last frame meets its first (a strike knocks a tentacle back to its start pose).
- Filler between bigger posts; little information, high rewatch.

## F15. "I just added…"
- 0-1.5 the result first: the new thing on screen. Text: what it is, in a few words.
- 1.5-10 it working in a real fight.
- 10-14 one honest line on why (post text on X).
- A daily build gives material every day, but this format draws developers more than players; keep the player-facing payoff in the first 2 s.

## Use with care
- POV clips ("POV: you're the last one standing at 4 AM"): only if the camera truly sells first person; the frame is tired.
- Grey-box → final before/afters, "N months of gamedev in 1 minute", prototype evolution: they can work (one case, Paper Sky, pulled thousands of wishlists in a day), but they show unfinished or cut content. Decide as a project whether you allow them.
- Trending licensed music: no (rights and Content ID).
