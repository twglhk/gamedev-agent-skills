# Short-form clips

Load `video-craft` first (pipeline, tools, QA gate). This skill decides **what the clip is and how it is cut**. Beat sheets for every format: [references/formats.md](references/formats.md).

## What a short is for

- Until the Steam page exists, a clip earns follows and tells you which hooks hold. Short-form is a booster and a test lab; the demo, festivals and streamers sell games (`video-craft` [channels.md](../video-craft/references/channels.md) "Expectations").
- Find the levers that travel for your game. Fear reads in a second, and so does a striking image. Deep strategy doesn't read in a feed, so a clip shows a feeling and one visible consequence, never the rules.

## The hook

- **Frame 0 already shows the thing**: a strike mid-swing, a monster's eye opening, a number racing up. No intro, title card, dialogue window, logo or slow establishing shot. Every documented miss opened with one of those.
- 3 s retention is similar across clips; what separates hits is the **3→5 s drop**. The second beat must pay off or escalate what frame 0 promised.
- Hook types: an impossible image; a number in motion; a threat line ("It can't die."); a question over the picture ("Which one is the hero?"); the result first, then how.
- Any series tag lives in the post text or title, never in the first frame.

## Length and framing

| Channel | Cut | Frame | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | 15-45 s | 16:9 or 4:5 | autoplays muted; the post text carries the words |
| Reddit | 15-45 s | 4:5 (16:9 for desktop-heavy subs) | title read first; mobile crops 9:16 to about 4:5 |
| YouTube Shorts | 15-30 s | 9:16 native | sound on; the first second is the thumbnail |
| TikTok / Reels | 9-15 s | 9:16 | sound on |

- Make one master per moment and cut each channel's version from it: same footage, own framing and own text.
- Vertical comes from a **native 9:16 camera** where possible; cropping 16:9 keeps 31.6% of the frame. Compose tall: threat in the top third, the clash in the middle, the player's side above y 1380. Fallbacks: a tracked crop on one subject, or a stacked split (boss above, team below) ([tools.md](../video-craft/references/tools.md)). Blur-fill only for wide shots, and it reads like a repost.
- Loops: if the last frame can meet the first, make it seamless. Replays count, and a hidden detail earns rewatches.

## Cutting a short

- One idea, one moment. Cut on events; hold the payoff. 1.5-3 s shots in a hype cut, 3-6 s for one readable mechanic.
- For card games: a card choice and its consequence in one shot or two adjacent shots, card art big and held ≥ 1.5 s.
- For horror: normal → wrongness → hold → sting → the response. One sting per clip, after silence, with a visual twin.
- Sound on, survives muted: SFX on each key action (about one every 2-3 s), licensed audio only, −14 LUFS.
- On-screen text: ≤ 6 words a line, one line at a time during action, in the post's language, inside the safe box. On X the words mostly go in the post, and the video stays pure picture.
- End: cut on the high point. An end card lasts 0.7 s at most; the follow or link ask goes in the post or a reply.

## Choosing the format

Pick from [formats.md](references/formats.md) by the material you have and the feeling it serves:

| Material | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| A fight with a big hit or a combo | F1 cold-open climax · F2 number goes up · F3 synergy reveal · F6 chaos escalation |
| Unit or card picks and a fight | F4 building the team |
| A discovery or hidden-information mechanic | F5 hidden in plain sight |
| A loss or a last stand | F7 the wipe · F8 clutch finish |
| A run that shows the loop | F9 "would you play this?" · F10 the game in 15 s |
| One character in a real setting | F13 tone clash · F14 micro loop |
| A new thing in today's build | F15 "I just added…" |
| A comment asking something the game does | F12 comment reply |

Run each format 8-10 times before judging it.

## Checklist (on top of the video-craft QA gate)

- [ ] Frame 0 is the strongest moment, and 3-5 s pays it off.
- [ ] One idea; the choice and its consequence are both on screen.
- [ ] Each channel's file is cut for that channel (frame, length, text language).
- [ ] Muted at phone size, the sentence "it's the game where…" comes to mind.
- [ ] At most one sting; the clip ends on a payoff.
- [ ] Not the same footage as a recent or queued post.

## Reading the result

Read at 24 h and 7 d: X views and % watched, Reddit upvotes in the first 2 h and comment quality, Shorts stayed-to-watch and average % viewed. Compare with your own median, never with one viral case. A curve that cliffs early means the hook failed: recut the hook, don't repost the same file.
