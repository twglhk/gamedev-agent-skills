## Set 16: Lore & Legends (2025)

48 traits (26 origins incl. unique Assimilator, Blacksmith, Caretaker, Chainbreaker, Chronokeeper, Dark Child, Dragonborn, Eternal, Harvester, HexMech, Rune Mage, Soulbound; 22 classes incl. unique Ascendant, Emperor, Glutton, Heroic, Huntress, Immortal, Riftscourge, Star Forger, The Boss, World Ender). Launch patch 16.1, December 3, 2025. Mechanic: 40 Unlockable champions. Numbers are the wiki's (last edited May 2026) and may include post-launch tuning; Riot's Nov 2025 preview differed on a few (e.g. Yordle 2 = 50 HP/7% AS, Zaun 10% Durability, Shadow Isles 18/20/22/25%).

35 traits (20 origins incl. unique Bulwark, Dark Lady, Doomer, Eradicator, Factory New, Galaxy Hunter, Gun Goddess, Oracle, Redeemer; 15 classes incl. unique Commander, Divine Duelist, Party Animal). Launch patch 17.1, April 15, 2026. Mechanic: Realm of the Gods (replaces carousel). Origin/class membership and origin breakpoints follow Riot's launch overview; class numbers are the wiki's (edited Aug 2026) and may include later tuning. ## Set 18: Enchanted Wilds (2026)

37 traits (23 origins incl. unique Attuned, Avatar, Bounty Seeker, Caustic, Emerald Aspect, Greenfather, Old Growth, Thornmaiden and hidden Eclipse; 14 classes incl. unique Apex Predator, Monolith). Newest set found. Launch patch 18.1, August 26, 2026 (live through at least patch 18.5, Oct 20, 2026). Mechanic: Wisps (Charms successor); carousel returned. Numbers are the wiki's (page flagged "incomplete", edited Sept 28, 2026) cross-checked with Riot's July 27, 2026 preview; post-launch patches 18.1–18.4 may be reflected (e.g. mid-patch Riftbeast 7 stats 6% to 5%). ## Revivals and re-runs

- K.O. Coliseum (Set 15) re-ran as the temporary mode "Choncc's Treasure" on patches 17.3–17.4; same traits as Set 15, no new traits found.
- Lore & Legends (Set 16) re-ran as "Choncc's Lore & Legends" from patch 17.7 and became "Choncc's Classic Treasure" in 17.8; same traits as Set 16, no new traits found (only patch headlines read).
